Operational Technology (OT) Security Patch Management System

My group utilised NuttX RTOS, MCUboot, and ESP32-S3 to perform Over-The-Air (OTA) updates, with security measures including encryption and hash verification before deploying updates to the target device.

The image shows the project's workflow simulation, using virtual machines and ESP32-S3 devices acting as publishers, susbcribers, and brokers.
