Final Year Project

Operational Technology (OT) Security Patch Management System

As part of the final year project, my group was tasked to utilise NuttX OS, MCUboot, and ESP32-S3 to perform Over-The-Air (OTA) updates.

We also had to ensure security measures such as encryption and verify hash values before performing an update on the target machine.

The image represents the project's workflow simulation via virtual machines and ESP32-S3s acting as publishers, susbcribers, and brokers.
